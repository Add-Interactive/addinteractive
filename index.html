    Skynet Nexus is a modern, family-first news station celebrating STEM, youth innovation, robotics, and discovery. Built for curious minds ages 5 to 50, our mission is to connect families through stories of real achievement and wonder in science, technology, engineering, and creativity.

    Our Audience

    We're built for families who value learning together. Parents and educators discover fresh perspectives on youth achievement. Young people see themselves and their peers celebrated as innovators and creators. Every story bridges ages—co-reading experiences that spark conversation and curiosity across generations.

    Agentic Newsroom

    Our editorial engine operates through coordinated AI agents—intelligent correspondents that discover, verify, and surface stories of youth leadership in STEM, robotics, and innovation worldwide.     Native Grant Hub

    Indigenous AI infrastructure

    Supporting Native American STEM Leadership

    Native Grant Hub is an Indigenous-focused AI grant support platform designed for Native American campuses and schools. We empower tribal institutions to access federal and private funding for critical AI infrastructure—GPU servers, workstations, storage nodes, and network switches—while maintaining full data sovereignty and cultural control.

    Mission-Driven Funding Access

    We believe tribal institutions deserve equal access to world-class AI infrastructure. Native Grant Hub streamlines the path from grant discovery to successful deployment, helping schools and campuses secure the hardware and connectivity their students need to compete and lead in STEM fields.

    Data Sovereignty at Every Step

    Your data belongs to your nation. Our platform is built with sovereignty first—ensuring tribal ownership of AI models, cultural datasets, and research remains protected. We provide guidance on compliance, privacy frameworks, and tribal data governance to keep your intellectual property secure.

    Step-by-Step Grant Playbook

    From SAM.gov UEI registration to Tribal Council Resolutions, CAGE codes, vendor quotes, and post-award compliance—we guide you through every stage. Our playbook turns the complexity of federal grants into achievable milestones, designed specifically for tribal institutions.

    Interactive Hardware Calculator

    Estimate server costs, match them to eligible grant opportunities, and visualize your infrastructure roadmap. Our calculator helps you build a compelling case for funding while ensuring your technology choices align with your institution's long-term vision.

    Join the Movement

    Native Grant Hub connects tribal leaders, educators, and students with the infrastructure and support they need to build sovereign, thriving STEM programs. Together, we're advancing Indigenous leadership in AI and technology.
